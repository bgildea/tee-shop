        How we make them

        • Drawn from real places

          Each design is a spot you can visit: a summit, a beach, a park bench in Bangkok. No generic sunsets.

        • Printed when you order

          Nothing sits in a warehouse. Your tee is printed for you, which is why it takes a little longer.

        • Made in Thailand

          Designed in Bangkok and printed by a Thai print partner on 100% cotton.

        Find your size

        Start with your height and weight. If you are between sizes, or like a looser fit, go one up.

        Measuring a tee you already own works best: lay it flat, measure across the chest from armpit to armpit, and from the top of the shoulder to the hem.

        Height and weight ranges are a guide for a regular fit. Shirt measurements are typical for a 100% cotton tee and will be updated with our print partner's exact blanks.

        How ordering works

        1. Order

          Add tees to your bag and tell us where to send them. You get an order number straight away.

        2. Pay by PromptPay

          Scan the QR code on screen, or the one in your email, with any Thai banking app. The amount is already filled in.

        3. We print it

          Once the payment shows up, your tee goes to print. Printing takes 7 to 10 working days.

        4. It arrives

          We post it with tracking, and it takes another 3 to 5 working days to reach you. Allow about 3 weeks from order to door.

        Questions

        What are the tees made of?

        100% cotton, regular fit, crew neck. The designs are printed straight onto the fabric, so they stay soft.

        How big is the print?

        Front prints are 25 to 28 cm wide, across the chest. Small chest prints are about 9 cm. Each tee lists its exact size and shows it against a ruler.

        What if it doesn't fit?

        Each tee is printed for you, so we can't take back the wrong size. If it arrives damaged or misprinted, email us a photo within 7 days and we'll send a new one.

        How do I wash it?

        Inside out, cool water, no tumble dryer. Don't iron over the print.

        Can I pay another way?

        Not yet. PromptPay works with every Thai bank app, and card payments are coming later.
