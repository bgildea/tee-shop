    Find your size

    Lay a tee that fits you well flat on a table. Measure across the chest, armpit to armpit, and from the top of the shoulder to the hem. Pick the size closest to your numbers.

    How ordering works

    1. Order

      Add tees to your bag and fill in where to send them. You get an order number straight away.

    2. Pay by PromptPay

      Scan the QR code on screen, or the one in your email, with any Thai banking app. The amount is already filled in.

    3. We print it

      Once the payment shows up, your tee goes to print. Printing takes 7 to 10 working days.

    4. It arrives

      We post it with tracking, and it takes another 3 to 5 working days to reach you. Allow about 3 weeks from order to door.

    Questions

    Why only small prints?

    A small print looks good with everything and doesn't crack across your chest after a few washes. We think it's the better tee.

    How big is the print?

    Each design lists its width. Most are 4.5 cm or 6 cm wide. Open a design to see it against a ruler.

    What if it doesn't fit?

    Each tee is printed for you, so we can't take back the wrong size. If it arrives damaged or misprinted, email us a photo within 7 days and we'll send a new one.

    How do I wash it?

    Inside out, cool water, no tumble dryer. Don't iron over the print.

    Can I pay another way?

    Not yet. PromptPay works with every Thai bank app, and card payments are coming later.
